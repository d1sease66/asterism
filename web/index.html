      FAQ

      Where does the data come from?

      GMGN's public smart-money and KOL feeds, token info and top-trader lists, read through its OpenAPI. Every trade carries its Solana transaction hash and links to Solscan.

      Why is the site 15 minutes behind?

      The Telegram bot gets every alert the moment it fires. The site shows the same data with a delay, so it never front-runs the people who use the bot.

      What exactly triggers an alert?

      Three or more distinct A/B wallets buying at least $300 of one token within 30 minutes. A KOL in the same window marks it as a stronger signal. An A wallet's first buy of $1,000+ and three A/B wallets fully exiting a signalled token also alert.

      Which tokens are filtered out?

      Market cap outside $30k–$20M, liquidity under $15k, younger than 10 minutes, mint or freeze authority still active, or more than 30% of volume from bundlers. The same alert for a token is sent at most once an hour; if the cluster grows, the original message is edited.

      How are wallets ranked?

      score = 0.45·hit rate + 0.25·rank of 30-day PnL + 0.20·win rate + 0.10·freshness. Top 15% are A, the next 25% B, the rest C. Until a wallet has ten buys with a known 24-hour outcome, early entries into winning tokens stand in for the hit rate.

      Can it trade for me?

      No. Quorum only reads public data. It holds no keys, signs nothing and never swaps. Nothing here is investment advice.
